FILE No. 0001 Filed Aug 16, 2026 Source: Nature Aging, Aug 14 2026

"Adiponectin is the real youth elixir — exercise stops ovarian aging."

— claimed in a viral short-form video, 2026

Partly Overstated

The underlying research is real and recent: a 150,000-person analysis links activity to later menopause, and mouse experiments show adiponectin is required for exercise's protective effect on the ovaries. What the video skips — this is an early, mostly associational finding, not a reversal of aging or a proven human treatment.
