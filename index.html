FILE No. 0004 Filed Aug 16, 2026 Source: Maturitas, Feb 3 2026

"Protect your muscle during menopause to protect your blood vessels."

— claimed in a viral short-form video, 2026

Verified

A 15-week randomized trial found resistance training increased muscle volume by ~4% in postmenopausal women, with no change in controls — building on established physiology linking muscle loss to rising cardiometabolic risk after menopause.
