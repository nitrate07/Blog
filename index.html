FILE No. 0002 Filed Aug 16, 2026 Source: JACC, Aug 11 2026

"Belly fat threatens your heart no matter what your BMI says ."

— claimed in a viral short-form video, 2026

Verified

A brand-new JACC study on more than a hundred thousand adults backs this up almost exactly: people with a "normal" BMI but a high waist-to-hip ratio showed up to 50% higher cardiovascular risk. BMI alone misses what belly fat distribution reveals — in both directions.
